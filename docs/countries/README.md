## What will I learn?

Each country guide explains:

1. How to identify your business using local registration information.
2. Which business details to include in your ABIZ record.
3. How to publish your record.
4. How to connect your domain to your record.
5. How compatible AI assistants can find and check your business information.

You can use ABIZ whether or not your business has a formal company registration number.

## About ABIZ

ABIZ (Agentic Business Record) is an open standard for publishing structured business information.

It helps compatible AI assistants find and check the information a business publishes.

ABIZ does not independently prove that a business is legally registered.

Read the [ABIZ overview](../../README.md) or the [technical specification](../../SPEC.md) to learn more.
