# Agentic business records in Asia

These guides explain, country by country, how a business in Asia can publish an agentic business record (ABIZ): a DNS TXT record on its own domain that points to a public JSON record of its business facts, so that compatible AI tools can find and check them. They follow ABIZ 1.1 (proposed). The [specification (SPEC.md)](../../../SPEC.md) is the authoritative text: if a guide and the specification differ, the specification applies.

Pages are written in the country's main business language where we could do so to a good standard; the others are in English for now. All example businesses, numbers, domains and keys are fictional. Never copy an example `p` value.

Countries under sanctions or with a high-risk environment have no to.co example; their pages show a self-hosted record only. ABIZ does not prove legal registration and is not an endorsement.
