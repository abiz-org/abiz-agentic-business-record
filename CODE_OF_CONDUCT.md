# Code of conduct

This applies to issues, pull requests and any other discussion in this repository.

- Be civil. Disagree with the argument, not the person.
- Stay on the subject of the specification and its tools.
- No harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- No advertising. Saying that your software implements ABIZ, where it is relevant, is welcome.
- Do not post anyone's private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block a person who keeps breaking them.

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, or contact a maintainer through the repository. Reports are handled privately.
